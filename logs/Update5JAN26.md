Update (Very Late)

The main issue was that the holes kept warping, since that section of the mount was being printed vertically.

Today, I realized that I overshot the dimensions for the strip that the cameras screw into. At this point, I’m deciding whether to cut that section off or just reprint the part entirely.

Once that’s resolved, I’ll move on to the electrical work, followed by the coding phase to get the system moving. After that, I’ll begin camera calibration, and then continue iterating from there.
